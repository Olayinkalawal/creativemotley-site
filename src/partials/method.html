Method

Everything we make passes through one instrument, and one way of working.

  1. Front elementThe first glass the light meets.
  2. Lens elementsGroups of glass that bring the image into focus.
  3. ApertureOverlapping blades that set how much light gets through.
  4. Lens mountA bayonet with electrical contacts: lens and body talk through it.
  5. Body and gripDials, shutter button and the hand that holds them.
  6. Viewfinder (EVF)A small screen behind an eyepiece: the frame, at the eye.
  7. BatteryA removable pack. Carry a spare.
  8. ShutterTimes each exposure, just in front of the sensor.
  9. Image sensorWhere light becomes a picture.
  10. Stabiliser (IBIS)In-body image stabilisation: the sensor moves to cancel shake.
  11. Main boardThe processor that turns what the sensor sees into files.
  12. Rear screen (LCD)For framing, menus and playback.
